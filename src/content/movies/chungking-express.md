It's really two short films stitched together, and most people end up preferring one half over the other — mine's the second, with Faye Wong sneaking into Tony Leung's apartment to rearrange his life while "California Dreamin'" plays for what feels like the fifth time that scene alone.

The handheld camera and step-printing during the chase scenes shouldn't still feel this alive thirty years later, but it does. Wong Kar-wai makes loneliness look like a mood you'd want to live inside rather than escape from.

The pineapple-can monologue at the start is one of the best openings to any film I've seen, and it's barely trying.
