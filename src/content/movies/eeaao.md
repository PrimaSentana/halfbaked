The hot-dog-fingers universe gets all the attention in clips online, but the film's real trick is how it keeps circling back to something small and specific: a mother and daughter who can't talk to each other. Every absurd universe is in service of that one relationship, never the other way around.

Michelle Yeoh holds the whole thing together. Whatever the film throws at her tonally — slapstick, action, quiet devastation — she never looks like she's performing a different register, it all reads as the same person.

The googly eyes bit shouldn't work as well as it does by the end. It really does.
