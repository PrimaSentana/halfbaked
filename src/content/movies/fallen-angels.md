Originally meant to be a third story in Chungking Express before it outgrew that and became its own film, and you can feel that lineage — same city, same restless energy, but pushed into something colder and more nocturnal.

The wide-angle lens distortion during the hitman's scenes gives everything a slightly off, alienated feel that fits the character perfectly. Michele Reis playing his partner-he's-never-seen-in-daylight is one of the more quietly devastating setups in any Wong Kar-wai film.

Less immediately charming than Chungking Express, but it lingers longer once it's over.
