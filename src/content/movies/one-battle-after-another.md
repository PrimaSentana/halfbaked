Coming off a run of films that mostly sit still and watch people talk, this one moves — genuinely tense set pieces, a plot that keeps the pressure on instead of drifting into vibes. It's still recognizably his film underneath, just with a different gear engaged.

What stuck with me most is how it handles the passage of time between the earlier chaos and the later fallout. The film trusts the gap to do emotional work instead of over-explaining what everyone's been doing since.

Loud in the middle stretch, quiet where it counts. One of the better theatrical experiences I've had this year.
