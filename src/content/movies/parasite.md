What gets me every rewatch isn't the twist in the basement, it's the staircases. Bong keeps shooting people moving up and down between floors, and by the time the flood hits you realize the whole film has been a geography lesson in class.

The tone shift in the second half still catches me off guard even knowing it's coming. Comedy curdles into something else so gradually you can't point to the exact cut where it happened.

Worth watching twice: once for the plot, once to watch how much information is hiding in the production design.
