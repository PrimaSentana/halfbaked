Every Geese release so far has been a hard swerve from whatever came before, and this one keeps that streak going — messier, more unhinged in the arrangements, less interested in a clean hook than their earlier stuff.

It rewards patience more than immediacy. First listen felt chaotic almost to a fault, but the structure underneath the noise reveals itself by the third or fourth pass.

Not an easy entry point to the band, but a rewarding one if you already know what they're capable of.
