After three albums of deliberate coldness this one lets actual warmth in, and it's a better fit than I expected going in. "Weird Fishes" builds for four minutes and earns every second of it.

Not their most inventive record, but maybe their most listenable front to back, which counts for a lot on a rainy commute.
