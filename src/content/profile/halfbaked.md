---
title: "Wtf is Halfbaked?"
---

Most review sites want to sound certain. Star ratings pretending to be objective, verdicts delivered like a jury's decision. Halfbaked isn't that.

The name is the whole idea — these are half-formed thoughts, written close to the moment of watching or listening, before the opinion's had time to fully set. A rating here isn't a final judgment, it's just where I landed that day.

"Be who you are and say what you feel, because those who mind don't matter, and those who matter don't mind."

— Bernard M. Baruch
